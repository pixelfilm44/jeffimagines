How I Build

Three rules I don't break

🚫

No Ads Mid-Play

Ads interrupt flow. My games don't have any — ever, not even between levels.

🧩

Real Challenge

Puzzles worth solving, physics worth mastering. Difficulty is a feature, not a paywall.

⏳

Respect Your Time

No energy timers, no pay-to-skip, no guilt-trip notifications. Play at your own pace.
