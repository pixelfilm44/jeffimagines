Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the screen stop moving when I land?

That is the core of our turn-based mechanic! It gives you a moment to catch your breath and plan your next launch. The action only advances when the frog is in the air.

Why do I have to pull left to jump right?

We use realistic catapult physics! Just like a real slingshot, the direction and power of your launch come from the tension in the opposite direction. It's a skill you'll master in no time.

What happens when I collect 10 Tadpoles?

The game will temporarily pause to offer you a choice of two powerful abilities. This is your chance to heal up or boost your jump power to reach higher scores.

Is there an end to the swamp?

No, the swamp is an endless, procedurally generated gauntlet that gets faster and more dangerous the further you travel.
