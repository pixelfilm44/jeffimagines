Support

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the goal of the game?

To reach each goal in the right order and then reach the finish line.

How do I move my frog?

Tap any highlighted pad next to your frog. You can move in all 8 directions, including diagonals.

What are the numbered goal pads?

Visit each goal in order (1, 2, 3...), then hop to the exit pad to complete the level.

What happens when I run out of moves?

Game over. Each level has a limited number of hops, so plan your route carefully. But you can try as many times as you like.

How do I deal with enemies?

Snakes chase you one step each turn. Bees fly in 8 directions every other turn. Logs drift down one row per turn. Avoid them or stomp them from trampolines.

Do I have to beat my own level before sharing?

Yes. You must complete your level to prove it's solvable before submitting it to the community.

Can I save a level draft and come back later?

Yes. Use the save button to store drafts locally. Load them anytime from the editor.
